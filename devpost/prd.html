Audience contract

These labels are product behavior, not decorative filters. Each establishes a different explanation promise.

Student

Warm and analogy-heavy. Assumes no domain background. Explains what and why.

Executive

Crisp and outcome-focused. Assumes business literacy. Explains so what and what to do.

Developer (Non-ML)

Technically precise. Assumes software fluency, not ML fluency. Explains how it works with engineering analogies.

Behavior reveals

Open a section to inspect the requirements behind the screen states.

Input and audience selection

The text remains editable at all times. Student is selected by default. Empty submission shows “Please paste some text to decode.” without starting generation. Changing audience preserves input and existing results.

Streaming success

Decode becomes “Decoding...” and disables. Four skeleton cards appear immediately while the input stays unlocked. TL;DR, The Analogy, Jargon Buster, and The One Thing to Remember fill in progressively and in that fixed order.

Stale results

Editing the source after a result exists leaves the current result visible and adds “Results out of date.” A later Decode uses the current source and audience.

Partial recovery

Completed cards stay. The active failed card becomes an inline error with “Retry this section.” A second failure exposes “Retry full decode.” The final takeaway always has an explicit recovery path.

Global failure

If the service is unreachable, the results area becomes one retryable error state. The source text and selected audience are never cleared.
